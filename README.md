# Baseline Blood and Cerebrospinal fluid predictors of Bacterial meningitis


## Project Overview 

this project uses a meningitis dataset to investigate whether a baseline blood and cerebrospinal fluid findings can help distinguish bacterial meningitis among patients with acute Meningitis. 

The clinical motivation is that bacterial meningitis require antibiotics treatment, while viral meningitis does not respond to antibiotics. waiting for definitive culture results may delay clinical  decision-making, so baseline laboratory findings may help support early diagnosis. 

## Research question

Among patient with acute meningitis, which baseline blood and csf findings are associated with  bacterial rather than viral meningitis? 

# General Objective

to investigate baseline clinical laboratory predictors of bacterial meningitis among  patients presenting with acute meningitis 



## Specific Objectives 

1. To describe the baseline demographics, blood, and CSF characteristics of the study population 

2. to compare blood and CSF findings between bacterial and viral meningitis
3. to calculate derivative diagnostic indicators such as CSF/Blood glucose ratio
4. to assess the association between selected baseline predictors and bacterial meningitis 
5. To develop and evaluate a simple logistic regression model for predicting bacterial meningitis. 


## Dataset 

the dataset contains patients with acute viral or bacterial meningitis. The main outcomes variable is 

abm = 1: acute bacterial meningitis
abm = 0: viral meningitis


the dataset includes demographic variable, variable test results, CSF Test results, prior antibiotic use and diagnostic outcome.
